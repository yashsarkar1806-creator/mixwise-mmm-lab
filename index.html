Last-click is broken, and big-brand MMM is out of reach

Since iOS 14.5, SKAdNetwork and cookie loss, Meta, Google and Apple Search Ads each report their own version of the truth. Add them up and you get more signups than you actually had. Open-source MMM tools (Robyn, Meridian, PyMC-Marketing) are excellent, but they need a data scientist and weeks of setup. Agencies quote enterprise budgets.

Who it's for

  • Growth leads at Series A–C consumer apps (fintech, edtech, health, subscriptions) in India and Australia
  • SaaS marketers running 4+ paid channels with no analyst
  • Founders who need to defend a budget split to their board

How it works

Upload

A weekly CSV with date, KPI, spend per channel and optional event flags (Diwali sale, EOFY, IPL, Black Friday).

Model

Adstock captures carry-over and Hill curves capture diminishing returns. A ridge regression with non-negative media effects then separates base demand from paid media.

Validate

Holdout error on the latest 13 weeks, bootstrap ROI ranges, and a check against your geo or platform lift tests.

Act

A Scale / Hold / Trim verdict per channel, a budget optimiser for the same spend, and a readout you can paste into a deck.
